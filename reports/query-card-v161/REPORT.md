## 1. False premises

1. **Jonathan Marsh's and Daniel O'Rourke's "Nudged 3 Oct" is not in the seed.** In the emulator seed (the harness
   account, copied 2 Nov) neither query (`cv25-q-09`, `cv25-q-04`) has a `nudgeDate` or a `lastNudgeSentDate`. The
   writer saw them on dev, whose data has moved on since; this pack does not touch dev's data. The fix is the rule,
   so it applies to those cards on dev as to any other. Their before and after shots look the same here.
2. **Step 0.3 found the reverse case too.** The prompt expects queries with a `lastNudgeSentDate` and no nudge step.
   - The seed has those only among the test fixtures: `q152-nudgedquiet` and `q152-nudgedwait`. Their threads now
     draw the nudge from the field.
   - The harness seed has the opposite: two queries with nudge steps but no `lastNudgeSentDate`. `rj-nudge`
     (Tamsin Probewell) has five; `rj-dupe` (Tobias Hark) has one.
   - Their threads said "You nudged" while every summary said they were never nudged. I gave each the date of its
     latest nudge step (see §3).
3. **"A passed reminder means nudge due" can mean a future reply date.** For a reminder that has come while the
   reply date is still ahead, the nudge-due footer's existing words ("Reply was due {date} · N days ago") would state
   a day still to come as past.
   - **Footer:** it reads "Reply expected by {date}" in that case. Those are the waiting state's existing words, so
     no wording is added.
   - **Grid card:** it keeps "Response overdue / Send nudge now", the wording the prompt names. That isn't true while
     the reply date is ahead; it's ruling 1.
4. **A nudge logged today did not count until noon, before v161 too.** `stepTime` (lib/qc152Write) stores a step at
   noon of its day, and `nudgeMsOf`'s "not in the future" guard compared instants. A nudge logged at 11:18 was in
   the future, so QC161-5 read "Reply was due 12 Nov". The old fallback had hidden this: before v161 the footer
   showed the check-back reminder instead. The guards now compare days.
5. **QC161-12's "outer box equals the baseline" cannot hold for the reply journey.** Its contents change by design:
   five tiles and a line take more room than the chip row. The drawer sizes to its contents under the same 66% cap.
   The box check holds for the other five journeys; for reply the lock checks the paper and the shadow, and that the
   box only grows.
6. **The save's block cannot be brought under 100ms without a restructure.** It is the whole app re-rendering: every
   workspace page stays mounted, including the hidden Dashboard, whose layout effect forces a layout. §5 has the
   detail; the motion now runs after the block.
