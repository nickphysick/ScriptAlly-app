2 · THE DOCK — HOW A TASK ACTUALLY GETS DONE — your 30/70: Action (from card, hover or ⋯) docks the item. Left: the queue as slim rails, the docked card ringed in ink, Esc returns to the board. Right: the work surface — band, title, the timeline from the activity log, and the REAL flow inline (here, recording the full sent: choose what goes, one ink act). Completing the flow ticks the card, animates it to Done with its undo toast, and offers the next in the queue. Derived tasks can never be dragged to Done — the dock is how they finish; a Done-drop bounces with “Sending the full is what completes this”. User tasks alone tick by hand. “Work the list” on Today = this dock, walking today’s queue.
