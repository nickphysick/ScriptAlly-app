Next up: the journey, for every kind of task

Your two questions have one answer: the track is the fixed four-stop track from the Queries cards (queried, partial, full, offer), so it never gets longer however much has happened. Here is the lead card for each kind of task.

  • Four stops, always, running edge to edge. The ringed stop is where the query stands; one line beneath says what and when.
  • Things that are not a stage (nudges, a revision request) never add a stop. They change the label under the current stop and the sentence.
  • The card head and tint take the stage colour. A task about several queries shows the agents instead of a track; a task about no query has a plain head and no track.
  • The sentence is what you do in the real world; the button is what you then do in QueryHawk. Every button opens the slide-out at that step, filled in and waiting. Nothing is saved until you confirm there.
