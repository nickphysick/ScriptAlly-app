Submission packages

Bundle your materials once, then send them without rebuilding each time.

Fed up of guessing which materials are landing with agents?

A package is one covering letter, one synopsis and one sample, bundled under a name. Every package keeps its own scorecard — ScriptAlly records which letter, synopsis and pages went to each agent, so the answer sits on the page, not in your head.

Managing your packages with ScriptAlly

Gather your materials, bundle them, and let the replies report back.
PKG-STAGE-ADD Stage one
Add your materials

Versions of your covering letter, synopsis and sample pages — written here or pasted in. Most writers keep two or three of each.

PKG-STAGE-BUNDLE Stage two
Bundle them into packages

Pick one of each and name the combination. Attach a package when you log a query — its contents go exactly as they are.

PKG-STAGE-TRACK Stage three
Track what comes back

Replies land against the package that went out. Tracking shows which materials sit behind each response and request.
