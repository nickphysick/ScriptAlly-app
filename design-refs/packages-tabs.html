What tabs win. The page stops growing downward. Tracking is where a writer spends time once the setup is done, and it currently sits below a ledger that gets longer every time they build a package. Two tabs keep both at the top.
What tabs cost. The reason to read “Prologue-first: 3 from 12” is that you are looking at the package carrying it — a click between question and answer. And on a new account Tracking is empty for weeks; an empty tab is a worse first impression than a section that simply is not rendered yet. Both are fixable: the ledger already carries per-package counts, so the comparison survives on Builder, and Tracking can stay hidden until the first send rather than showing an empty state.
The tab labels carry counts — 4 · 5 and 18 SENT — so the other tab is never a blind click, which removes most of the cost of splitting them.
