What changed. The pinned block (eyebrow · title · controls · chips · rule) is flex:0 0 auto and compacted to ≈200px by tightening vertical gaps only — no control moved or resized. Everything below is one scroll region: flex:1 1 auto; min-height:0; overflow-y:auto, with min-height:0 on every wrapper between the page root and it. That min-height:0 is the whole bug — without it a flex child refuses to shrink below its content, the region reports its full content height, and the surface clamps to one screen.

Sticky heads. Each group head is position:sticky; top:0 inside the scroll region, so it pins whilst its own rows pass and is pushed out by the next head. The head carries an opaque white background and a hairline via ::after, so rows never bleed through it. Snoozed and Done stay folded at the foot.

Composer. Height is content-driven — the tag row is no longer clipped. A footer carries Cancel and Save, Save disabled until the title is non-empty, Enter saves, Esc dismisses. It opens at the top of the scroll region so the pinned controls stay reachable.

Pro banner. Unmounted. Worth noting its figures were wrong anyway — 44 tasks against an Outstanding of 16, and 43/15 in the second screenshot. Whatever it counts, it isn't the list.
