Folder tab rules (normative)

1 · Full names while they fitThe current page is always the paper tab, named in full. Its sibling pages follow as recessed tabs, also named in full, as long as they end at least 24px short of the search field. The biggest groups today are Materials and Tasks, at three pages each.
2 · Then iconsWhen the next name won’t fit, the remaining siblings drop to icon-only tabs, using the same icons as the sidebar, with the page name as a tooltip. A tab never truncates mid-word.
3 · Then “+N”If even the icons won’t fit, the last tab becomes “+3”, which opens a small paper menu of the rest. The current page is never hidden in the overflow, and the order is always the sidebar’s, so tabs don’t reshuffle as you move between them.
4 · Re-fits on every changeCollapsing the sidebar, resizing the window or switching group re-measures the space and settles in one frame, without animation, so tabs never jump about while you watch.
5 · Search never movesSearch is a fixed 168px field at the start of the right-hand group, 12px before “Log a query”. The tabs fill the space up to 24px short of it, and nothing else in the bar ever moves.
The real fix for big groupsA group that needs eight tabs probably needs splitting in the sidebar too. These rules make sure the tab never breaks; they don’t make an eight-page group a good idea.
