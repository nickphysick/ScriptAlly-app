One card per relationship. A relationship is one card from its first send to today (still running, faded) or to its named date. The status changes along the way — request came in, nudge went out, reply landed — are marks riding on the card, in the app's own dot grammar, not cuts through it. The text starts after the last mark. Fragments are impossible by construction: there is nothing to fragment.

The content ladder. Each card measures its own width after render and shows the most it can hold without clipping: full (pill · headline · detail) → headline (pill · headline) → pill only → stub, a status-dot-sized disc in the card's colour. Anything below full carries the rest on hover. Nothing is ever cut mid-word. Switch to six-monthly to watch cards step down the ladder; Whitfield and Osei reach the stub.

The row head carries the real StatusDot at 18px with 14px of air beside it — the locked component, imported, never redrawn. Today sits at 50% so the board shows as much past as future. The action column is gone.
