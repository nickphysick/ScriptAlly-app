“One click and you’re on the list”: on brand

Why today’s email looks off: Gmail, Outlook and most phone mail apps don’t load web fonts, so the typewriter heading falls back to plain Courier, and nothing else in the email says QueryHawk. The fix is to carry the brand in two small images that always look right, the pass and the heading, and keep everything else as real text. Both proposed views below are shown as Gmail renders them (no web fonts).

What changes

  • The pass, half-finished. The email opens with the founding writer pass reading “Almost yours” and a dashed “One click to go” stamp. The welcome email then shows the same pass completed with their number, so the two emails read as one journey.
  • The heading is an image in the real typewriter face, with the navy rule. Its alt text is the same words, so it still reads when images are off.
  • Bigger button with an arrow, navy as on the site.
  • The raw link moves to the bottom as “Button not working? Copy this link…”, instead of sitting under the button where it looked like a mistake.
  • Signed by you: “Nick, Founder, QueryHawk”, as in the welcome email.
  • Same fix for the welcome and waiting-list emails: their pass is HTML today, so “No. 14” also falls back to Courier in Gmail. Pre-render the pass as images: one per founding number (No. 1 to No. 50) plus “Waiting list”, 51 small files, and the email picks the right one. The headings become images too.
