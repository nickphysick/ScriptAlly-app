What was clunky, named
  • The page said "24 queries" twice — masthead and list heading. The guidance is one title over the whole split; panes don't introduce themselves. The list's heading is gone; the masthead says it once.
  • The masthead was an island — a centred card with ~120px of dead white each side and content starting ~310px down. It is now a slim band across the whole split, and the working area starts ~150px higher.
  • The list floated in a page gutter. It now runs flush from the masthead to the foot on a faint ground tint, one hairline from the pane — the split reads as furniture, not as two cards on a page. Selection inverts: rows sit on the tinted ground and the selected one lifts to white.
  • Proportions were off. The list takes ~340px (~a third at 1200 of working width) against the guidance's balanced split; the pane stops being the leftover.
  • The hero was a box in a pane of boxes. It becomes a band under the masthead — same grammar as every flattened header on this page — and its facts (queried date, days waiting) move onto its line, which lets the Tracking card stop half-repeating them.

A · The ledger — keeps the three sage cards but disciplines them: equal heights from a shared grid row, a footer line on each (status / count-plus-action / write-a-note) so no card trails off into white, and interior density evened with the same label-value rhythm in all three. Least change from what's built.

B · The dossier — the pane becomes one sheet: the query's file. Sage caps move to the left edge as section labels; Tracking, What you sent and Notes are ruled sections of a single document with the sheet's two-line cut edge. No box-in-box anywhere, reads top to bottom like a record card, and it rhymes with the journey sheet — the journeys lay a sheet on the desk; at rest, the selected query is a sheet on the desk. My pick, and the honest reason against it: it re-cards what we just de-carded, so it must be the only drawn surface in the pane — hero stays a band, nothing else boxed.
