Rejections are part of querying

Almost every published writer heard no many times before they found their agent. A rejection is rarely a verdict on your book. More often:

  • It isn't to this agent's taste, and no book suits every agent.
  • Their list already has something close to it.
  • It reached their desk at a busy time, or just before they closed to queries.

When a no comes in, record it and it's listed here, with a mark on your tally.
