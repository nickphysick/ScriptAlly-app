How to send the setting back to me
  • Drag until it looks right, then hit ⧉ Copy settings and paste the block into chat — or just read me the line under the sliders. It names every value the prompt needs: image, size, offsets, opacity, rotation, and the letterhead mark. Nothing else about the sheet is affected by these controls.
  • Offsets are from the sheet's bottom-right corner, and negative values bleed off the edge — which is what makes it read as printing on paper rather than a picture dropped in a box. The default (-70 / 34) is the version I recommended.
  • Watch the queue rows as you raise opacity. Past roughly 0.10 the speed-lines start cutting through the collapsed What and Notes rows; that's the practical ceiling regardless of how good it looks in isolation.
  • Toggle to Record before you settle: the same geometry has to work with the sage letterhead, and the plane hue-rotates to match. If a setting only works in the pink register, it isn't the setting.
  • Rotation is there for one reason — if the flight angle fights the dock's horizontal, a few degrees settles it. If you find yourself past about 15°, the placement is probably wrong rather than the angle.
