The silence gets a figure. 2 years past the window they stated — the scaled formatter, measured from the stated close, in Playfair so it reads as the card's one finding. It only renders when a window was stated; without one there is nothing to be past, and the card says so instead. The phrasing keeps the fact on the agency's side of the line: past the window they stated, not "overdue".

The future appears as a ghost rung. When a nudge reminder exists on the to-do list, the timeline shows it where it will happen: dashed ring, dashed connector, faint ink, dated in the future. The record above is solid because it happened; this is hollow because it hasn't. It links to the task, and it answers the question a writer actually has in a long silence — is anything going to happen, or is this drifting?

When nothing is scheduled, the offer carries the next step instead — three equal-standing actions: Nudge now, Remind me later (creates the to-do task, which then appears as the ghost), and Mark closed. The offer only appears when there is genuinely nothing pending: no unanswered recent nudge, no reminder. One prompt, facts first, never repeated after dismissal — same rules as the closure offer.
