Where I land: B, shingled. Each card overlaps the one above by 18px, so the band and identity of every card stay readable while a column of eight takes the height a grid gives three. Hover lifts a card clear and shows its fact line; click opens the drawer. It's the "fanned hand" idea without losing the name on any card.

A is the safe version — clipped to identity, no overlap, roughly twice the density of the grid. C goes furthest — 52px per card, heavy overlap, the column fans open on hover — and reads as a deck of tickets; wonderful at 30 queries, but the leaf and agency vanish at rest and the fan-open motion is a lot for a scanning view. Columns: Queried · Partial Requested · Partial Sent · Full Requested · Full Sent · Offer, with Closed as a narrow grey column at the end so it's present but not competing. R&R sits in Full Requested's column as its own band (it's step 3 of the same hue); No response sits in Closed.

No drag between columns. Moving a card would be a status write, and status is derived. The board is a read; the verbs stay in the drawer.
