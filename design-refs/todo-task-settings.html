How it lands in the packs

Tags are a colour-law amendment to the retoken pack (burgundy fill → the two-depth pink system; burgundy retained for StatusDots and micro-accents only). The fork + Task settings is a new small pack: the fork loses its never options and gains the settings doorway; the settings page is a UI over the existing mutedTaskRules (+ per-item flags) with the Restore list — mute still deletes nothing, and now it's visibly undoable. Requests/deadlines and offers are always on, and Dismiss never renders on Urgent cards: a task board that can be told to hide your own deadlines quietly stops being trustworthy. Dismissing fires a toast — “Hidden — restore any time in Task settings” — so the way back is named at the moment of hiding.
