How to use this

  • Press the ringed button on each screen, or press Autoplay to watch it through. The arrows and the arrow keys step without playing.
  • The review steps respond: settle a duplicate, answer the date question, and the counts update.

What's different from the build

  • "That's everything captured" is gone. Your dashboard loader ends on the dashboard, and the set-up sidebar becomes the app's nav as it does.
  • Skipped rows: tick "A row was skipped" (top right) to see the one-off line the dashboard shows. With nothing skipped, the dashboard says nothing extra.
  • The last review button reads "Import and get started", your build's wording. The stepper's fourth step is the loader itself.
  • The dashboard is yesterday's v58, drawn as the destination and not redesigned here. Its drawings are left out.
