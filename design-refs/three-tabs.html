Packages. One tile per package: contents, note, and the three counts at 25px. Tiles snap; dots and arrows navigate; the last card is a ghost that opens Builder. A note-less package offers ＋ Add a note, an unsent one shows the idle line instead of three noughts.
Builder. No ledger at all — parts on the left in two columns, the bench sticky on the right. The rail gets twice the width it had, so chips can sit two-up and still read. Drag or click; the bench is the only build surface.
Tracking. Package against package across the top, each with its request rate and its contents — muted where a value is shared, weighted where it differs, so what actually varies between two packages is visible without reading both. The per-version and per-material panels sit beneath.
Worth saying plainly, since I argued against this shape: the differential treatment on Tracking is only possible because packages sit side by side rather than in rows — it is a better answer to “what is working” than the ledger's columns were. What the split costs is cross-highlighting, which needed the rail and the ledger on one screen.
