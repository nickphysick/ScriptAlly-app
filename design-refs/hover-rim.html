What's happening
  • A 0.5px border on a transformed element rasterises at a fractional offset. The top edge rounds to nothing; the sides, landing on whole pixels, survive — which is why only the top disappears.
  • scale() resamples the border itself, so even a full 1px rim thins under it.
  • The hover shadow's top band paints over whatever hairline is left.

The fix
  • Draw the rim as inset 0 0 0 1px, not border. Inset shadows are painted inside the element's own raster and survive transforms and fractional positions intact.
  • Never scale() a bordered surface. Translate only, and in whole pixels — -2px, not -1px.
  • Promote the layer up front with will-change:transform and backface-visibility:hidden so it rasterises once instead of re-snapping mid-transition.
  • Separate rim from lift. The inset shadow is the rim; the drop shadows are the lift. They no longer compete for the same pixels, and the rim can darken slightly on hover as part of the response.
  • Check for an ancestor with overflow:hidden — a 2px lift needs 2px of room, or the top is clipped rather than resampled. Different cause, identical symptom.

C is the safer default. A shadow-only response reads as a lift without moving anything, so there is no transform to go wrong and nothing to clip. Reach for B only where the movement is doing real work.
