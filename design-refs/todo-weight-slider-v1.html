Stale-query tidying is exactly the thing a writer wants out of sight for a season. The full range is honest here.
Why weight by group, not by row. The page already sorts urgency into groups and states it in the head — An agent is waiting, or a date is against none of it urgent. Weighting per row would duplicate that judgement in a second place and eventually disagree with it. One rule: filled inside Needs you now, outlined everywhere else. Both weights keep all four guards and the identical 118px footprint, so the column stays aligned and nothing shifts as a row moves between groups.

The slider is not new machinery. CLAMPSNOOZE already exists in todoActions and already knows the ceilings — the slider reads its clamp rather than hard-coding one, so offer rules stay in one place. CHECKBACKSLIDER from the nudge flow is the same day-then-week control; extend it with the month stops rather than building a second slider.

Keyboard. The slider takes focus on menu open but fires nothing — arrows move a stop, SHIFT+ARROW jumps a unit, Enter commits. That keeps "snooze till tomorrow" at open-then-Enter, which is what the number keys were protecting. Keys 1 and 2 are therefore not needed and should not be added to KEY_MAP — one less entry for the ? overlay to answer.
