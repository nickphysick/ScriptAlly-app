The packaged attachment, three cuts

The current strip fails as an object, not as an idea. Faults, named: the dashed placeholder reads as broken until artwork lands; the materials are bare text with form-label eyebrows, not the pills the loose state uses; the actions sit inside the object, turning a record into a settings panel; and the blue is so pale the Pro surface reads as a disabled input.
All three cuts share the fixes: deeper blue (ink #39587a on #e3ebf3→#d5e1ec, edge #b9cdde), solid marks instead of dashed placeholders (the commission stands — art swaps in later, but the interim must look finished), the same pills as the loose row in white-on-blue, and actions outside the object, hover-revealed like the pane's other in-place edits. Hover each to see them.

Option A · The tag

A label tied to the send
The strongest object. An eyelet and a dashed tear-line make it a parcel tag — something tied to a shipment, which is literally what it records. The eyelet echoes the sidebar's punch-holes, so it borrows an existing house motif rather than inventing one.
