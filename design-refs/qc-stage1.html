My pick: B. Full width, 112px tall, faint ruled lines on parchment that scroll with the text — it says “write here” the way a notecard does, and it’s the one place a handwritten register belongs. A is the safe choice; C adds chrome for no gain. All three fix the real fault, which is the box not filling its step.
