Top-nav shell v3 — hover-open, morphing mega, graded rules

Hover a nav item (~100ms intent delay) and the section rolls out: height unfurls on a spring curve while the content rises and fades in, the bar's hairline strengthens to #ddd2c2, and the page dims behind. Now slide the cursor along the nav without leaving: the surface never closes — its height morphs between sections while the outgoing pane slides out and the incoming pane slides in from the direction of travel (left-to-right when moving right, mirrored coming back), and the parchment pill tracks the cursor. Move into the panel: stays open. Leave the whole region: a 160ms grace closes it — no flicker at the boundary. Click still toggles for touch; Escape and the scrim close as before.
