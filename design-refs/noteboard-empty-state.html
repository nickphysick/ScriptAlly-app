Your board is empty

Querying throws a lot at you at once — an agent’s wishlist heard on a podcast, a line that finally works, a rule you keep forgetting. This is where those live until you need them.

One

Write it down

A quote, a comp title, a half-formed pitch. Pin it in a second and get back to what you were doing.

Nothing here goes anywhere else unless you send it.

Two

Colour and tag it

Three papers and a tag of your own wording. Filter to #agents when you’re polishing a letter, or search the lot.

Your tags, your grouping — nothing is prescribed.

Three

Give it a date, if it needs one

A note with a date turns up on your to-do list and calendar when it’s due — and stays here on the board. One note, not a copy.

Undated notes stay put. They never chase you.

Or keep one of the examples below to start with.
