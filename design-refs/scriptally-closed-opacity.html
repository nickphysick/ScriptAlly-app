Dim the shut doors

The rule

  1. Sage band = something live. Soft pink = nothing live. The pill says which.
  2. Closed door = hatched band, ink pill, and the card dims. You cannot submit, so it recedes.
  3. Unless something of yours is already live with them. A shut door does not make your outstanding full less important — that card keeps full strength and shows the hatch only.
  4. Hover restores full opacity on any dimmed card, so nothing is ever unreadable when you go looking.
