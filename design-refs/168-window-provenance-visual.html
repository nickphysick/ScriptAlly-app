No start, no bar. ⚠️ And the sentence is inverted even when the fault is real: it has a closing date, it lacks an opening one. Card 4 is how that state should read, with the offer that fixes it.

The distinction, currently, is one pronoun. Priya advises versus you expect, plus a small end label. That is a lot of weight on a single word — and when the bar fails to draw, as here, the word is the only signal left.
