How it works

  1. Add titles to your shelf that are comparable to your manuscriptBooks, films and shows like yours, each with a line on why it fits.
  2. Record which query letter references which titlesTick the comps each letter mentions. Once a letter is sent, its comps can't be changed.
  3. Track their success and shape your strategySee which comps went out with the queries that got agents asking for more.

Two or three comps in a letter is usual. Keep more on your shelf to choose from.
