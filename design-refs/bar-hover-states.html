The hover contract

  1. Target is the bar. Entering the bar's box (or its peek) starts the state; leaving both ends it. The row, the lane and the gap between bars do nothing.
  2. Comfortable: lift + reveal. The bar translates 2px up-right; the row's action (Caveat label + outlined button) fades in at the bar's end and the event symbol wakes to ink. Ghost stages go to full opacity and nothing else. No tooltip, no summary pill.
  3. Compact: peek. After 60ms of intent, the Comfortable card renders over the bar as a page-layer overlay (shadow, never clipped, flips upward when out of room) with the same action reveal; the bar beneath dims to 40%. Ghosts peek at full opacity. Clears on leave, on scroll, or when the pointer enters another bar.
  4. Urgent bars never hide their pulse. The dot on the today line is always visible; the action is hover-only.
  5. One state at a time. Only one bar can be lifted or peeked; opening the drawer (click) clears any hover state.
  6. Hover never navigates or writes. Nothing happens to data on hover; the action button is a real button and needs a click (which opens the Action sheet).
  7. Touch has no hover. On touch, a tap on a bar opens the drawer directly; the action button sits in the drawer.
