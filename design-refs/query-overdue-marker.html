The overdue marker

Same card, same sentence, five markers. All ink or brand pink — no red, no amber.

On sand: I'd take A — sand for Queried only. Queried is the majority of any live gallery, so giving it the quietest tint keeps the wall calm and lets sage mean "they've asked for more" — which is the moment a writer actually cares about. B's ladder is more systematic but Partial Sent and Full Sent as one sage lose their distinction from the requested states anyway (the dot carries that). Closed is nudged a touch greyer (#e9e6e1 → #dfdcd6) so it never reads as sand.

On the marker: option 1, the ink ring. It's the same weight and register as the lucide line-icons already in the chrome, so it reads as part of the app rather than a badge, and it sits well beside the sentence at 16px. Option 4's pink pill is the alternative if you want overdue to shout "your move" — but the sentence already says it, and pink pills next to a pink band (with-you) would blur.
