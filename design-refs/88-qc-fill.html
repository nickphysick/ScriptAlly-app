Rest state — the rhythm exception, and where the command bar went
  • Content starts 18px below the top bar. A deliberate page-scoped exception to the shared rhythm: this is the most-visited workspace, and the masthead now carries its facts inline — 23 queries · 12 awaiting reply — instead of spending a line on a subtitle that described the page to someone already on it.
  • The command bar splits by what things act on. Search, filter and sort act on the list — they sit in one slim strip under the masthead. View tasks and Nudge act on the workspace — right end of the same strip. Agent, Manuscript and Delete act on the selected query, so they move into the hero row beside Record response, behind a kebab where they always belonged. The old toolbar was three scopes flattened into one row, which is why it read as clutter.

The journeys — an arc instead of a void
  • A place line under the title. "Your 17th query for Murphy's Day Out · 12 currently awaiting reply" / "You sent this 1 day ago · the 4th reply you've recorded." The journey states where this act sits in the larger one. Reporting, never appraising — no streaks, no encouragement, just the fact of the campaign.
  • The dock: a desk edge, not a floating footer. Cancel and Save anchor to the bottom of the viewport with a hairline and a slight blur, so the page never "cuts off" — the composition runs top edge to bottom edge regardless of how short the content is. The consequence line lives in it: pick an outcome in Recording a response and watch "Saves as Partial requested · your turn" arrive where the commitment happens.
  • The open ground carries a watermark — the quill at 5% opacity, bottom-right, behind everything. The empty space stops being an absence and becomes margin: deliberate, papered, owned. It appears only in journeys, never behind the working list.
  • Header chips move to the header's right, where the actions used to be — the actions are in the dock now, so the header carries identity and progress, the dock carries commitment. Two jobs, two places.
  • Motion note for the prompt: the dock slides up 8px with the entry stagger's final beat, and the place line fades in with the header block. On save, the consequence line is what the receipt confirms.
