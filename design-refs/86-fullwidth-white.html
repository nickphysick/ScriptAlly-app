What actually changes — and what doesn't
  • The surface stays white. No recolouring. The change is that the working area stops being a drawn object — the hairline rim, radius, card padding and shadow around the whole list-and-pane region go, and the content takes the page with a 24/30px gutter.
  • Structure comes from rules and gutters instead of a box. One hairline under the page header. One vertical hairline between list and reading pane — that seam is real, because the two sides scroll and select independently. The toolbar keeps its rule. Nothing else is drawn.
  • Real objects keep their containers. The hero, the three sage-headed cards, the glance panel, the all-queried block, the collapsed steps — each is a thing whose boundary means something. What goes is only the box around everything, which meant nothing.
  • The takeover loses its floor. The current card carries a min-height — the screen of dead white beneath the steps in your screenshot. With no container, nothing is sized to the viewport: the page ends where the content ends.
  • One header on the takeover. The QUERY CENTRE / Close chrome bar goes — toggle back to carded on the takeover view to see it return. Cancel and Esc already do that job, on the header that owns the journey.
  • Width is now honest. The reading pane's three cards get back the ~80px the nesting was taking. At very wide viewports, cap the content region at ~1560px and centre it rather than letting lines run the full glass.
  • The page header also flattens. The Query Centre title was a white card floating above another white card; it becomes the page's own masthead — title, subtitle, actions, one rule beneath.
