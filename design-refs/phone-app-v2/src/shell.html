QueryHawk on the phone

Try these

  1. Tap a query on Queries, then drag the sheet up and down.
  2. Swipe a query row left or right.
  3. Tap the pink + and log a query, one question at a time.
  4. Tap the + again and choose An agent replied. Pick “Asked for the full”.
  5. On To-do, tap a task’s circle, or swipe it left for Later.
  6. Tap your initials, top right, for everything else.
  7. On Home, switch the chart between Active, Sent, Replies and Requests.
  8. Scroll Home and watch the tab bar tuck away.
Open this page on your phone and the prototype fills the screen. Swipes and sheet drags work with a finger there and with a mouse here. Everything is example data; nothing is saved. Press Reset to start again.
