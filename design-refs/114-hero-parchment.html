Synopsis
The agent header in parchment
  • What it gains. The pane becomes one material family — parchment for every label surface (the card headers, the header plate), white for every working surface (the card bodies, the list). That is a clean rule, and it means the plate stops being the only white-on-white element with a shadow doing all the separating.
  • What it costs — and this is the thing to judge. The plate was made a contained, raised object three packs ago precisely because it needed to read as the subject the cards belong to. Give it the same ground as the card headers and it becomes a peer of them: four parchment surfaces in a column, one of which happens to be bigger. The lift is now the only thing saying which is the parent.
  • Three ways to hold that. White plate keeps it as built — the parent by material and elevation. Parchment keeps the lift but changes the ground, so elevation alone carries the hierarchy. Parchment + card rim drops the lift entirely and makes it flatly one of the family — cleanest as a system, weakest as a hierarchy.
  • My read: parchment with the lift kept. It buys the material consistency you are after without giving up the one signal that says this is the thing the cards describe. If it reads flat in the browser, the fix is more lift, not a return to white.
  • The avatar goes white on parchment for the same reason it does in the list — a pink disc needs a ground it can sit on, and parchment is close enough to pink to muddy it.
