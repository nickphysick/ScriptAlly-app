The clipping was a line-height bug, not a design problem. Playfair's descenders — the y and p in Murphy's — need more room than a 1.14 line-height gives them, and the row's align-items: baseline plus the card's clipping layer cut what overflowed. Fixed here at 1.35 with 1px of bottom padding. Any underline makes this worse, so it has to be fixed before a link treatment goes on.

Button alignment. Every set above is one height (42px), one label size, one glyph size — including Log new query, which is the thing currently out of step. The rule to hold: one height for every button in the bar, set by the tallest thing in it, and the pink button is not an exception.

On the sets. 1 is what is deployed. 2 puts pink on Record response, which now conflicts with Log new query — two pink primaries on one page is one too many. 3 reads strong but reintroduces the near-black pill we removed from the band. 4 ties the primary to the card headers. 6 groups the three closing verbs into one segmented control, which is the only set that shows the left/right split structurally rather than by gap. 9 reduces Delete to an icon, the cheapest way to stop the destructive verb being the widest thing on the right. My picks: 6 for the structure, or 9 if you want the destructive verb quieter — and 1's rim-weight primary in either case.

On the manuscript name. A hyperlink is right — it goes somewhere, and nothing else in the card does. D is my pick: underline on hover keeps the two names identical at rest, which is the whole point of the icon-disc treatment, and reveals the affordance exactly when it is relevant. A is the honest always-visible version if you would rather it be discoverable without hovering. E is out — burgundy on the title was already tried and rejected. G stops it being a name at all.
