The rules that keep it honest
Defaults are stated, never silent — the receipt shows exactly what was logged before you move on. Offers are exempt — an offer of representation can't be quick-anythinged; it always gets the moment. Notes just tick — nothing to log. Dismiss is a stance, not a delete — snoozed a week, back on the board after.
