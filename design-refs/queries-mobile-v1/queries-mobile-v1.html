Queries on a phone, take three: bringing it to life

Same flow as take two, dressed the way the best-loved apps do it. What they share, and what this borrows:

  • A character that reacts (Duolingo’s owl, Headspace, Finch): the hawk appears at moments that matter: the header, the top job, waiting, celebration. Never on every row.
  • Chunky, pressable controls (Duolingo): buttons and chips sit on a solid bottom edge so they look tappable, in the brand’s rust and navy.
  • Celebrate progress (Duolingo, Strava, Monzo): a full-screen moment when an agent asks for more.
  • Warm texture, not chrome (Airbnb, Headspace): the doodle paper behind the header only, stamps and dashed “post” lines as the visual language, rounded friendly shapes.

Real hawk art from the app is used where it fits; dashed boxes are placeholders for new pieces to brief your illustrator.
