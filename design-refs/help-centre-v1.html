What's wrong with the Help centre today

Read from the dev line. The structure built in October is good: search, guides you can resume, browse by area, a status key, and articles with "show me" links. The problems are mostly in the content.

Content

  1. 26 of the 27 articles are still marked as drafts. Only "Add an agent" has been signed off. All were written on 20 October.
  2. They describe an app that has since changed. Log a query, the Query Centre, Query letters, the navigation and today's rulings (founder's code, plans, the query clock, Settings) have all moved on.
  3. Whole subjects are missing: your plan as a founding writer, your founder's code and signing in, emails and reminders, changing the timings, Your notes and ranks, Query letters, Responses.
  4. Placeholders are visible to writers: "Illustration slot: The Archivist with a lamp" on the home page and "Screenshot slot: the add-agent editor" inside articles.

Look and rules

  1. Small mono capitals everywhere ("HELP CENTRE", "QUERIES", "QUICKER WITH A LIST"), the eyebrow style you've ruled out.
  2. Green. The "Learned" tag, the guide progress bars and the tip box are green or sage.
  3. Reply time disagrees with Contact: Help says "usually within a day", Contact says two working days.

What it could do better

  1. It doesn't use what it knows. A writer with three queries past their reply date sees the same home page as someone on day one.
  2. "Did this help? Not really" goes nowhere. It should ask what was missing and send that as a note.
  3. You have to be signed in to read it. Someone who can't sign in can't reach the article about signing in.
  4. No "what's new". Founding writers will send notes; there's nowhere that shows what changed because of them.
