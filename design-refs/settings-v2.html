What's wrong with Settings today

Read from the code on main and checked against the desktop UI principles. The parts themselves (label on the left, paper card on the right, ink toggles, pill buttons) are sound and stay.

The big ones

  1. It tells founding writers they're on Free and should upgrade. The rail's plan card, the foot row, the account menu and the Plan section all say "Free" with an Upgrade link. Nothing in Settings knows about founding writers, and there's nothing to upgrade to.
  2. Seven sections for about twenty controls. Profile holds two fields, Preferences two, Notifications three. Most pages are mostly empty sheet, and the writer clicks through seven places to see very little. The redesign has five, each with enough in it to be worth a page.
  3. It's correct but administrative. No illustration, no live headline, nothing of the brand beyond the typewriter face. The Archivist's job is records and "You", and this is where the Archivist should be.
  4. Two ways of saving, and a note to explain them. The name needs a Save button; everything else saves as you change it; a footnote explains the difference, and leaving the page with an unsaved name fires a warning toast. In the redesign everything saves the same way, with Undo.
  5. Feedback has no Undo and no stamp. A 2-second "saved" toast is the only response. The principles ask for the postmark stamp plus a snackbar with Undo.
  6. Green. The "Verified" and "Connected" chips and the success messages are green. QueryHawk never uses green.

Section by section

  1. Sign-in & security: "Change email" silently takes you to the public Contact page. It should either change your email or say plainly that you need to write to us.
  2. Plan & billing: "billing" with no billing. The plan is stated three times on screen (rail card, foot row, page).
  3. Notifications: the page says twice that emails aren't being sent yet (a banner, then a footnote), above toggles that therefore do nothing.
  4. Preferences: a three-theme switcher, though the app is now one look. A time zone picker that nothing reads.
  5. Tasks: its own section and its own row in the account menu, for six controls. "Decide" is explained in a footnote instead of being shown as always on.
  6. Your data: five cards, including one that holds a single sentence and one that is just Sign out. Export gives a JSON file, which most writers can't open. Deletion is still completed by hand.

Smaller things

  1. Italic footnotes at 13.5px and chips at 11.5px are under the agreed sizes; labels are 15px where body should be 16.
  2. Toggles are 22px tall, under the 24px minimum target.
  3. No designed loading, unconfirmed-email or deletion-scheduled view at page level (the banner exists but the page doesn't change around it).
  4. The account menu's "Task settings" row duplicates a page two clicks away; "Your notes" has no home.

Needs building, not just restyling

  1. A founding-writer state on the plan surfaces.
  2. A real change-email flow (confirm from both addresses).
  3. Name saving on blur, with Undo on every setting.
  4. Worth considering: a spreadsheet export alongside JSON, and automatic deletion before founding writers arrive.
  5. Added after comparing with other apps: sign out of all other devices, a "finished querying" way out in the delete dialog, a link back to Emails in every email, and Privacy and Terms links in Your data.
