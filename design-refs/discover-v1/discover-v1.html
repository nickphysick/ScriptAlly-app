As writers join, their agent lists build one shared catalogue. Every agent in it is checked by hand before the Scout brings you the ones who want a book like Murphy’s Day Out — with the reasons they fit. Nothing joins your contact list without you.

How it works.

  1. Illustrationa writer adding an agent card to a growing pile
    1

    Writers add the agents they’re querying.

    Every contact list on QueryHawk feeds one shared catalogue. Yours already has — nothing more to do.

  2. Illustrationthe Archivist with a stamp, checking a card
    2

    Each agent is checked by hand.

    A real agency, open to queries, never a reading fee. An agent only reaches the Scout once it’s been verified.

  3. Illustrationthe Scout with binoculars, holding out a card
    3

    The Scout brings you the ones that fit.

    A few at a time, matched to your manuscript’s genre and the agent’s wish list, each with the reason. You decide who joins your list.

The Scout never writes to an agent for you. It finds them; querying is still yours.
