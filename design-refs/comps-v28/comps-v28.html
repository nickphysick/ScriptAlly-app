— Comparable titles —

Every comp,
tracked

Build a library of comps for Murphy’s Day Out, add them to your query letters, and QueryHawk tracks which ones went out with the queries that got agents asking for more.

How it works.

  1. 1

    Add titles to your shelf that are comparable to your manuscript.

    Books, films and shows like yours, each with a line on why it fits.

  2. 2

    Record which query letter references which titles.

    Log the comps each query letter mentions. Once a letter has gone out, that record is fixed.

  3. 3

    Track their success and shape your strategy.

    Every query a letter goes out with is logged against its comps, so you can see which ones get agents asking for more.

Two or three comps in a letter is usual. Keep more on your shelf to choose from.
