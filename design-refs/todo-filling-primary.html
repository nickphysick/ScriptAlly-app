What the build needs to know

It looks disabled; it must not be disabled. A truly disabled button is a dead end — no click, no focus, nothing for a screen reader to explain, and no way to discover what is missing. So the faded state stays clickable: pressing it opens the first unanswered question and focuses it, exactly as the gate does today. aria-disabled="true" plus a live description ("4 questions still to answer") gives assistive tech the truth without removing the route.
The fill is proportional, not decorative — it reads the same required-fields declaration as the count, the steer square and the missing-answers line. One source, four expressions; if a journey has one required question the fill goes 0 → 100 in a single step, which is correct rather than a special case.
The fill recedes. Removing an answer through Edit runs the animation backwards. A progress indicator that only ever advances is lying about a form you can revise.
Motion: 340ms with a standard ease, and a single 3.5% scale nudge at the moment it becomes ready — the one "you can go now" beat. Under prefers-reduced-motion the fill jumps and the nudge is suppressed.
Bulk keeps its exception: its primary counts rows ("Log 6 queries") rather than questions, and its fill tracks touched rows against the cohort — at zero it is faded with an empty fill and its count reads "no queries filled in yet".
