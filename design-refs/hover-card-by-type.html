Does it hold?

  1. Yes for every type, with one card shape and two decisions made per type: which two dates bound the gauge, and which three numbers are honest.
  2. The gauge always reads sent/requested/created → expected/due/decide. When today is past the end it overruns in rose (sand for silences); when there is no end date the bar is the elapsed time; when the stage is finished there is no today marker.
  3. Counter one is always the headline number the eyebrow already shows (waiting, overdue, days to decide, days quiet, days left). Counters two and three change by type and may be a dash — never a guess.
  4. The note is the last note on the relationship (or a dash); tasks show their roll history there.
  5. Closed rows (rejected, withdrawn) use the ghost form: full gauge, no today, counters = total days · nudges · outcome; no action.
  6. It is compact-only. At Comfortable, the card is already on screen; the hover there stays lift + action.
