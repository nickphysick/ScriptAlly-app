The To-do list on a phone

This brings the desktop To-do (v52) to the phone using what's already been picked for the phone: the navy band, white cards, the floating tab bar, one question per screen, and the query card as a pull-up sheet. The tray becomes the list, the desk becomes a full-screen task, and the preview on the right becomes the card that builds in the band.

Everything on the three screens can be tapped. Tick the options, go back and forth with Next, tap the card in the band, and collapse a group.

3 · The card. The desktop's right-hand preview becomes a pull-up sheet. It's the same live Query Centre card the phone's query sheet already uses, with the dashed bubble showing what the task is about to log. Swipe it down to go back to the question.

What changes from the phone To-do on dev

  • The groups by when (Overdue, Today, This week…) become the desktop's groups by action.
  • Up next becomes Start here, the same task the desktop picks.
  • The week strip goes, now that the desktop has retired its calendar.
  • Tapping a task opens the desk's own questions (one per screen), not the Query Centre drawer's.

What stays as it is

  • Home keeps its short list of what's due, with "See all" opening this page.
  • Packages keeps the fourth tab.
  • Swipe for Later uses the existing snooze sheet, with Undo.
  • The navy snackbar after Log it, the Corkboard, and Housekeeping's Quick fill.
