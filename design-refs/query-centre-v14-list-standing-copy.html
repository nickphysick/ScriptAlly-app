2 · List row

Status is plain text with its dot — no pill — and the row carries a 4px left accent bar in the state's deep tone. Sent is the leaf, always the original send date, and always in the Queried sand — it marks the start, whatever the row's state now. Since then is a row of small event icons for everything that has happened since — requested ←, sent →, nudged (bell), response, closed × — in date order, each with a hover tip giving the event and its date; empty rows say nothing yet. Where it stands on an agent-side row reads Reply expected by {date} with the caption {n} weeks away | Nudge agent in {m} weeks — the divider is a hairline pipe; when no nudge is set the second half reads no nudge set; once the window has passed it reverts to Reply was expected by {date} — {n} days past / nudge available. It also gets a 44px round illustration slot, vertically centred, left of the sentence — a stage-specific spot (same family as the drawer header's), placeholder until artwork exists. Court becomes Actions: the contextual primary (Record response / Mark sent / Record decision), then icon buttons for Nudge (bell — the "snooze") and Mark closed (×, the "dismiss"), then ⋯ — on a fixed four-slot grid (132 · 30 · 30 · 30) so every row's buttons line up; absent verbs leave their slot empty. Every one opens the same desk the drawer uses, with the drawer opening behind it on that row's query. Nudge is absent on with-you and closed rows; on closed rows the primary is Reopen, greyed until the reopen flow exists.

4 · Motion

Scrim covers the whole viewport — portal to body, inset:0, over the rail and masthead too — fading 0 → rgba(58,28,20,.14) over 240ms.
Open: drawer translateX(102%) → 0 over 360ms, cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1) (fast out, soft settle, no overshoot); the header block and the body fade-and-rise opacity 0→1, translateY(8px)→0 over 260ms starting at 120ms, so the chrome lands first and the content follows.
Close: 220ms, cubic-bezier(.4,0,1,1); scrim fades in the same window; no content stagger on the way out.
Between queries (← →): the drawer stays; the body crossfades 160ms; the header block's tint transitions background-color 200ms.
Desk: unchanged (260ms slide from the drawer's edge).
prefers-reduced-motion: all durations 0, scrim still fades 120ms.
