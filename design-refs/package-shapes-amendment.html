The amendment

Two changes
1. Every slot except the covering letter becomes optional. Letter-only and letter-plus-synopsis are real submission shapes, so the builder shouldn't insist on more.

2. One free-text "Other" line per package — mirroring the Other row that already exists on the agent Materials tab. Catches chapter outlines, pitch documents, separate bios, writing CVs, anything odd, without committing the app to a taxonomy it would then have to maintain.
