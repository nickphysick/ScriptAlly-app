Where I land. Yes to all three, as a view switch in the toolbar — not nav items, not a title page. They're three lenses on one set: the grid for scanning, the list for triage (sort by any column, dense, twelve to a screen), the calendar for time. All three open the same drawer, all three obey the same tiles and filters, and the view persists per session. The stat tiles are the quick filters grown up — the counts were always the point; now they earn their space and the swatch becomes an icon disc in the ladder colour.

Two cautions. The calendar is the Tasks page's timeline board (`timeline-v57`) re-hosted with queries as rows rather than agent relationships — bars from sent to expected, solid when the agency stated the window, dashed when it's your estimate, pins for sent / requested / nudged, a burgundy today line. It should be the same component, not a cousin; if it can't be re-hosted cleanly, defer it rather than fork it. And the grid's tweak here is a compact card (smaller chip, 18px name, 42px leaf) — roughly 40px shorter — so three-up shows four rows where it showed three. If you'd rather keep the cards as they are, the switch works just as well.
