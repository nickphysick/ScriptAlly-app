Questions

What's the difference between Free and Pro?

Free covers the essentials: one manuscript, up to 20 agents, and every query and response tracked. Pro adds the tools that make querying smarter, from manuscript versions and submission packages to Smart email drop and The Scout.

Free covers the essentials: one manuscript, as many agents as you like, and every query and response tracked. Pro adds the tools that make querying smarter, from query letters and comparable titles to manuscript versions, submission packages, Smart email drop and The Scout.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every new account has everything in Pro for its first 14 days, with no card needed. After that you're on Free, and nothing you made is deleted.

Is Pro a subscription?

Yes, but a flexible one. You start with a plan that matches how long you expect to be querying, from one month to twelve. If you're still going when it ends, it carries on month by month at £7.99 until you cancel. We'll remind you a week before.

Hello, I'm Nick.

I started querying with a spreadsheet, like most people do. I work with spreadsheets for a living, and it still got away from me. Which version of the letter went to whom, which agent had said no reply means no, which nudge was already a week late.
