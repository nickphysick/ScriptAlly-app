The synopsis

610 words · last edited 18 May

MURPHY DOYLE, fourteen, has spent two years being the girl whose mother left. She keeps a jar of dead flies on her windowsill — one for every week since — and she has never told anyone why.

On the last Friday of term she takes a shortcut across the roof of the science block and finds a fly that is still moving. It is warm. It is heavier than it should be, and when she holds it to the light there is something written on its wing in letters too small to read.

By Monday two men in grey coats are asking her form tutor about lost property. By Wednesday they are outside her house. Murphy tells no one, because the only adult who has ever believed her about anything is DEREK PRYOR, the school caretaker, who has worked at St Aiden's for thirty-one years and has been waiting, he says, for someone to finally ask him what is under the floor of the boiler room.

What Murphy learns over the following week undoes the story she has been told about why her mother left, and about what her mother was carrying when she did.
