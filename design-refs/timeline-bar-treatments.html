How should a waiting window look?

The same seven rows in three treatments. Coloured = your move in all three; the question is what their move looks like when nothing is being asked of you.
What the pulse is claiming. Motion says live, happening now. A reply window is real time passing, so it isn't a lie — but eleven bars breathing at once reads as eleven things wanting attention, when the whole point of a waiting bar is that nothing is being asked of you. Weigh that against the calm it costs.

If it ships, it needs `prefers-reduced-motion` honoured (built in above — try the checkbox), a period slow enough not to register as a UI response (4.2s here), no motion on the your move bars (they're already loud), and no motion in past weeks, where nothing is live.
