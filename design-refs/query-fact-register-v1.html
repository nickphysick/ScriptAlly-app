The fact line, with a register

Same six cards, Notion styling, no well. Three ways to let the sentence carry tone — calm when nothing is asked of you, louder when something is — without a verdict word or a red anywhere.

The registers, and what each is allowed to do. Five: calm (with the agent, in window), late (with the agent, past the window), your move (with you), offer, closed. The rule that keeps this honest is that the sentence stays a fact in every register — "Reply was expected by 10 Sep — 1 day past" is louder than "Reply expected by 23 Sep" because of how it's set, not because a word was added. No "overdue", no red, no exclamations beyond the ring you already have.

My pick: A, weight and warmth. Calm queries set their sentence in muted Playfair italic — it reads like a note in a margin, the eye passes over it. Anything that wants you goes to Inter medium in ink, and the phrase that matters — the days past, "not yet sent" — takes a marker underline in the state's tint: sand-pink for late, the deeper pink for your move. Offer goes Playfair at reading size, because an offer deserves the good typeface. Closed fades. Six cards on a wall now sort themselves by tone before you read a word, and nothing on the card is a colour it wasn't already.

B is the same idea with a marker-pen block behind the phrase rather than an underline — clearer at a glance, a touch loud at 26 cards. C states the register in words on a chip (Waiting · Past expected · Your move · Offer · Closed); it's the most explicit, but it adds a fourth element to the fact line and it repeats the turn caption in the band.
