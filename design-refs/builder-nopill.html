Switch manuscript?

You are part-way through building a package for Murphy's Day Out. Its materials do not exist under The Smoke Test, so the bench will be emptied.

Nothing is lost — the materials stay where they are. Only what you have assembled here is cleared.
Fills, not inks. The card edge is a 5px band of the family's fill — pink #f3e0d6, sage #d7ddd5, blue #d5e1ec. Section heads and Add buttons take the same fill, so the colour a card carries is the colour its section carries.
The type pill is gone from the library card. Its section head says SYNOPSES six inches above it — repeating SYNOPSIS on every card was the label restating the heading. The name now leads the card, which is what you actually read.
It reappears in New package, where the card has left its section and nothing else says what slot it is filling. Same pill, same fill, same ink — so the card gains a label at exactly the moment it stops being obvious.
The manuscript control belongs on this page after all. I argued the opposite earlier, on the grounds that the shell states scope permanently — but that is true of a label, not a switch. Every material, every package and every figure here changes when the manuscript does, which makes this the page where switching is most likely and most consequential. It sits at the end of the tab row: the tabs divide this manuscript's work, the chip says whose work it is.
The scope bar states the consequence in words — every material and package here belongs to X — because "9 materials · 3 packages" changing under you is otherwise indistinguishable from data loss. The counts sit beside it so the switch is visibly a change of library rather than a change of filter.
Switching mid-build asks first. The bench holds materials that do not exist under the other manuscript, so it must clear — and a half-built package vanishing silently is exactly the class of fault this app keeps finding. The confirm says what is cleared and what is not: Nothing is lost — the materials stay where they are. With an empty bench it switches without asking.
The menu lists each manuscript with its own counts, so you can see what you are switching into before you go — including No materials yet, which is the honest state for a book you have not started querying.
