Why the rows don't move. Each question owns one 40px row whether open or answered — open, the pills sit beneath it; answered, the answer sits in the row's right-hand end. So the form's height changes only by the height of the open question's pills, and the answered ledger above it is stable to read and to click.

Edit is deliberately quiet — muted until hover, no icon, no button chrome. It is a correction affordance, not an invitation; the writer's eye should go to the open question, not to four ways of changing settled ones.

The record card fills its own height — status, three tiles, the story with the silences labelled, the query link at the foot. It no longer has to match the worksheet's height, because it is dense by nature and the worksheet is short by design.

What the strip still does: the answered rows now carry the facts, so "This records" shortens to the consequences the rows can't show — the two future dates. When nothing is answered it is an em-dash; it never repeats a row.
