Everything rare, plus a duplicate route to snooze and dismiss in plain language for anyone who never learns the glyphs. Destructive verbs sit last, below a dead zone and a rule. Nothing pre-focused on open.
Retokened. Primary is .b-soft-burg — pink fill, burgundy ink, 0.5px #E8C8BC border, mono at 0.07em — with the white ghost beside it. Burgundy stays an ink, a border and a track fill, never a button fill. Surfaces are parchment on kraft ground with the 1px RGBA(124,58,42,.28) inset border; hairlines are 0.5px #ECE0D2; every label is mono 9px at 0.17em in #9C8878. Popover heads take the To-do page's pink gradient band, not the sage one.

Why four icons and not three. The cluster is fixed-width whatever a row can do, so the column never reflows and each glyph is always in the same place.

The glyph problem, handled three ways. Every icon has a tooltip naming the deed in words and showing its key, so pointing at it teaches it. Only the first glyph varies. And the ⋯ menu duplicates snooze and dismiss in plain language, so a writer who never learns ◷ is never stuck.

Touch. On a coarse pointer the cluster renders permanently — a hidden control on a phone is an absent one.

What this retires. The split button, the 3px seam, arm-before-press, and the caret's 34px target. What survives untouched: SnoozeDial, the receipt through useTodoToast, the cardMenu → performCardVerb path, and the menu's contents.
