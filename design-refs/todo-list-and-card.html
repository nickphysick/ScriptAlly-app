The date folds into the where-it-stands sub-line, where it reads as a sentence. Rows drop from 110px to 74px; eight fit where three did. Everything else is the Query Centre's: Playfair for the headline and the person, italic agency, the dashed stamp, the pink verb, × and ⋯.
