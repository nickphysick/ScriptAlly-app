Getting started checklists

One pattern for every workspace page, so a writer learns it once. The checklist teaches by pointing, ticks itself when the writer does the thing, and never stands between them and their work. The Help Centre is its home base.

Try it. The Contact list screen has a working guide with these rules applied: auto-open, spotlight, the timed note, skip, and the finish.

01 · When it opensOpening

First visit
Opens by itself once per page, 1.2s after the page has settled (data loaded, no skeletons). After that, only the pill opens it.
One per session
Only one guide auto-opens per session. If the writer has already seen one today, the next page's pill shows a soft pulse instead. Six pages shouting on day one is what makes people press "hide for good".
Never over a task
It doesn't auto-open if the writer arrived mid-task: a modal or drawer open, a deep link to a record, a search already typed, or the Log a query journey running. It waits for the next quiet visit.
Empty state
On an empty page the empty state's own buttons do the teaching, so the guide doesn't auto-open. The pill is there. Items that need data show "Add an agent first" and are locked until then.
Coming back
Never re-opens itself. If items are left after 7 days, the pill pulses once on the next visit. That's the only nudge.

02 · The "done" noteHow long the note shows

6 seconds, then it shrinks into the pill and that segment fills. A thin line along the bottom counts down so it never vanishes by surprise. Hover or keyboard focus pauses it (needed for WCAG timing rules), Esc or ✕ closes it at once, and "Next" goes straight to the next spotlight. The last item is different: the finish note stays until the writer closes it.

03 · Getting rid of itDismissing

Yes, always dismissable. There are four levels, from lightest to strongest, so nobody has to choose "never" just to get it out of the way.

04 · What the best ones doThe things that matter most

Credit what's done
Tick items the writer has already done, including before the guide existed. If they imported 40 agents, "Add an agent" is ticked on arrival. Being asked to do something you've done is the fastest way to lose trust.
Show, don't do
Keep this. A row spotlights the real control and the real action ticks it. The checklist itself never performs an action.
Value first
Item 1 is the action that makes the page worth having. 4 to 6 items. Each item: 2 to 5 words of imperative, plus one plain line on why.
Locked, not missing
Items that need something first say what, in words: "Add an agent first". No greyed-out mystery.
Learn more
Every item links to its Help Centre article. Every article has "Show me in the app", which starts that same spotlight.
Announce
The popover is a dialog with focus moved in and returned to the pill. Ticks are announced through a polite live region. No pop animation under reduced motion.

05 · Above and beyondWhere QueryHawk can lead

  • Your guides, in one placeThe Help Centre opens with every page's guide and progress: resume, restart, or see which pages are done.
  • Ticks that travelFinish a step in the app and the matching article shows a tick. Read an article and press "Show me", and the step ticks once you do it.
  • A proper finishLast item: a sage "Learned" stamp, then "Next: Query Centre guide →". The pill turns sage for that visit, then retires.
  • Streaks & goals tie-inFinishing every page guide earns a "Knows the ropes" milestone on Streaks & goals (only if that page is switched on).
  • Help in the pageThe "?" menu leads with this page: its guide, its three key articles and search, without leaving the page.
  • Know where people stopLog each step's view, start, tick and skip. The step most people skip is the one to rewrite or remove.

    Add an agent

    Every query starts with an agent on your list. Add one by hand in under a minute, or bring a spreadsheet in all at once.

    1 min read·Updated 19 Oct 2026·

    See it on your Contact listWe'll point to the button. You do the rest.

    1. Open the editor

      On the Contact list, press + Add an agent in the header. The editor slides in from the right so your list stays in view.

    2. Name and agency

      Only the name is required. Add the agency and country if you know them. Each agent sits on their own, even if two share an agency.

    3. What they're looking for

      Pick genres from the list, and paste their wishlist in their own words. Genres power the "Ready to query" section.

    4. Response policy

      Choose No response means no or Always replies. QueryHawk uses it to work out when a query has gone past its expected date.

    5. Save

      Press Add to your list. Any detail you skipped shows up in List health, ready for later.
