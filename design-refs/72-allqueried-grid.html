What changed
  • One "Add a new agent" destination. The panel's action and the link beside the field fire the same handler — the quick-add form opens inline beneath the field, where the link already pointed. Nothing navigates, nothing duplicates.
  • The panel is 620px, not full width. It reports a fact and offers three ways out; it is not the page's subject. The three routes are compact actions on one line rather than three full-width cards claiming equal weight with the search.
  • Route labels lost their second lines — "Straight into your list" restated the label. The contacts route keeps its count because that is information, not description.
  • The heading drops to "Every contact queried" with the number in the ring, so the count is stated once rather than three times.
  • The gap this closes: the panel already says a resubmission is possible, but before this there was no way to act on it except typing a name from memory. The grid makes the sentence true.
  • Cards state what happened rather than judging it — awaiting reply, closed, offer, and the date. No ordering by rating, no nudging toward anyone.
  • The legacy second search field is gone. Lift the quick-add out of AgentSearchField so the pane can reach it without mounting a whole second combobox — that mount is the cause of the duplicate, not a styling problem.
