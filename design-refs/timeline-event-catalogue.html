6The flow, end to end

StepWhat happens
Select
Click any chip or band. It takes the burgundy ring; the focus band below fills, full width, nothing truncated. Selecting is free — nothing is written, nothing opens.
Read
Focus band: Playfair headline with the agent in italic burgundy, one context line, mono facts (sent, window, since, exchange), actions right.
Act
The primary opens the task pane in a window over the timeline — the same pane as the To-do list. Escape and × close; clicking the scrim never does, because the pane holds unsaved answers.
Commit
The write goes through the one completion primitive. One toast, with Undo. No receipt drawn on the row.
Settle
The card evaporates from every surface at once — timeline, To-do list, badge — because the deriving condition stopped holding. A record chip appears on the day it was logged. Any band restarts or ends.
Undo
Restores done and clears the completion timestamp; the card returns everywhere. Proven end to end.
Deliberately absent: auto-scheduling (a date only surfaces, never writes) · red or urgent states · the word "overdue" · verdicts of any kind · a second correction surface · any receipt the row can't draw.

Still open, in the order they matter: the four burgundy questions above · whether an ICS feed happens (it needs a revocable per-user token — a design decision, not a code one) · whether the month view survives as a secondary "far horizon", or the week pager is enough.
