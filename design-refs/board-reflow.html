The rules that make it safe. Freed width is claimed by the leftmost column whose content overflows the viewport; a column may span at most TWO lanes; reading order fills top-to-bottom then flows to the second lane, so the sort survives (item 5 sits at the top of lane two, not beside item 1). Because card order is derived by the sort — never stored — the lanes are pure presentation: dragging still targets the ordered list, and nothing about the partition or the counts changes. The head stretches across both lanes with the ink rule — the newspaper section spanning its columns, which is your editorial grammar doing exactly what it was born for. The “+5 more” fold remains after both lanes fill; lanes halve the scroll, they don’t abolish it. Un-fold Snoozed and the lanes collapse back to one — 220ms, the same curve as everything else.
