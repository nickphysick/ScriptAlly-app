The marker grammar, complete

Three markers, one rule each — and the shape tells you whether a fact exists. Every event the timeline can show is below, sorted by which marker it takes. Nothing is left over.

StatusDot

The query's status changed here. The locked app-wide component, at timeline size — ring fill for journey depth, colour for direction.

↑Direction dot

Something was recorded, but the status held. Smaller, no ring fill — an entry on the record, not a step in the journey.

Dashed flag

A date arrived. Nothing happened, nothing is on the record — so nothing is solid, and there is nothing to click through to.

One thing to check before this is built. StatusDot is specced at 12–13px and locked app-wide; here it renders around 30px. Confirm the component scales cleanly at that size, or give it an explicit size variant — do not fork it. If it cannot scale, that is a finding, not a licence to draw a second dot.
Why the reminder has no exclamation mark. A reminder falling due is the date you chose, arriving. An exclamation adds alarm the app has no business feeling — the same instinct as "overdue", ruled out on the same grounds. The dashed ring says a date, not a fact, and it reads as a sibling of the StatusDot rather than a warning.

The rule that falls out of it. Solid marker = something is on the record = clickable, opens the entry. Dashed marker = a date only = not clickable, nothing behind it. The interaction rule and the visual rule are the same rule, so neither can drift from the other.
