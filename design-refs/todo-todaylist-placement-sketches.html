My read

Sketch 2 (slim strip) is the sweet spot. It recovers almost all the height the corner pop-up would — the strip is ~54px versus the panel's ~214px — while keeping the two things that made Today's list worth having: your commitments visible at rest (as chips) and a live done count you watch climb. One click still expands the full committed/done log when you want it.

Sketch 1 (corner pop-up) wins only if you find you rarely look at Today while working the lanes — then hiding it entirely for maximum lane space is the right trade. Sketch 3 is tightest but the command bar starts doing two jobs and gets crowded; I'd avoid it unless vertical space is truly desperate.

All three keep the pink-glass identity in the bar, the full-detail cards, and the done-log concept — they only move where Today lives.
