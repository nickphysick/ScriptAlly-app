Packaged strip, floating materials

Option B, refined. A package is a contained object — blue-filled strip, parcel in the leading slot, name on the seal. Loose materials aren't contained at all: they float on the pane, sheets and chips, nothing boxing them. The distinction is structural, so the eye reads it before the words.

4 · The two illustration briefs

For the artist
Slot A · Package

a wrapped parcel, tied with string and sealed
Slot B · Loose materials

Shown when materials went without a package. Sits on the bare pane with no fill behind it; same 22px/38px constraint. Must not read as lesser — different, not worse.

two or three loose sheets, slightly fanned
Two drawings, same size and weight, so they read as a pair of categories rather than a filled and empty state. The parcel gets a coloured ground; the sheets get none — the container does that work, not the drawing.
