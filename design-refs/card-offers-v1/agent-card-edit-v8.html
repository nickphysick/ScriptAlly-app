What else moves

Today is 11 Nov. Sarah has one open query, The Lighthouse Keeper. Try:

  1. Offers, not errors. Move the response time and a sand card appears under the slider: the query, its reply and nudge dates old → new, and a plain choice. Update is the default.
  2. Keep means keep. “Keep them as they are” pins that query to the window it was sent with. Only future queries use the new one.
  3. Closed is smart. Within her window, we suggest leaving the query open. Past her date with “no response means no”, we suggest closing it as no reply. A full request in progress is never touched, and a calm note says so.
  4. Nothing happens until Save. The save bar repeats the choice; the card’s next step shows the result.
  5. The page dims. A little while you read the card, more while you edit. Click the dimmed page to close (it asks first if you have changes).
  6. + Website and + Email open Contact details with the cursor in the right field.
