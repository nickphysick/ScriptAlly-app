What's wrong with Plans today

Read from the code on main and compared with the public Pricing page and your October rulings.

It contradicts your own rulings

  1. The founding offer is the old one. The page promises "Free Pro through beta, then half price for as long as you need it". You ruled founding writers get a whole campaign free, with no founding discount.
  2. The price is wrong. It shows Pro at a flat £4.99. The public page sells 1, 3, 6 and 12 months at £7.99, £19, £29 and £45.
  3. Submission packages are shown as free. You ruled them Pro-only. Manuscript versions aren't listed at all.
  4. "The Comp Scout" where everything else says The Scout.
  5. Agents: this page says unlimited on Free; the public page says up to 20. One of them is wrong.

The public Pricing page has drifted too

  1. It still says "pass" and "No subscription", and its FAQ says a plan never renews. You ruled on 24 and 26 October: "plans" not "passes", and plans roll on to monthly at £7.99 with a reminder.
  2. It says you keep unused months if you sign. You ruled the remaining months simply run out.

What should be Pro (v2 recommendation)

  1. Free runs a whole campaign. Unlimited agents and queries, tracking, to-do list, reminders. No cap on either: your nearest rival tracks queries free.
  2. Pro is everything that sharpens the submission or saves time: letters, versions, packages and their results, comparable titles, The Scout, Smart email drop, monthly Smart Import.
  3. Every new writer's first 14 days are full Pro, then they drop to Free with nothing deleted.

The page itself

  1. "Choose your plan" with nothing to choose. A founding writer can't buy anything, and the founding card sits at the bottom, below a comparison that doesn't apply to them.
  2. Two near-identical columns. Most rows read the same in both, so the real differences are hard to spot.
  3. "Tools that think alongside you" reads as an AI claim, which the product avoids.
  4. Old parts. Sage and pink bands, burgundy ticks, a handwriting face and mono "Coming soon" pills, none of which match the app now.
  5. No help choosing a length in the app, although the app already knows the writer's list and pace.
  6. It's a stray page. It lives in the working nav's shell while everything else about your account lives in Settings.
