What changed
  • True sequence. The four steps sit in order and the active one expands in place, so completed steps are always above the one you're on and upcoming steps below. Nothing jumps.
  • The reference panel is now a persistent column spanning the whole body and sticky on scroll — not a sibling of the active step. It stays put while the flow moves beneath it.
  • Whitespace: the expanded step uses the full column width — When is three columns across, materials are three cards, the picker auto-fills. The card is sized by content at every stage.
  • Nudge me after now carries a custom date option using the locked calendar popover — the same component as Date sent. Picking a date clears the week chips and the derived line reports the interval back.
  • Stage 1 is dropdown + grid: the field opens a typeahead on the first keystroke (never on mount or focus) and searches every contact, queried or not; the grid beneath is a standing set of suggestions and does not filter.
  • The opening sample is a unit-aware stepper: arrows and ↑/↓ move in sensible increments per unit (5 pages, 500 words, 1 chapter), typing always overrides, and switching unit converts the figure rather than leaving a nonsense number.
  • Materials are the four locked rows — query letter, synopsis, unit-aware opening sample, free-text Other. Pre-ticked from the agent’s stated requirements and fully editable.
  • Completed rows carry their values and reveal an EDIT affordance on hover, so going back is explicit rather than implied by a click target.
