The four fixes

1 · Historic bars. v65 draws a past bar in its own state colour, faded — opacity .34 at rest, 1 on hover with a raised z-index — not white, not grey. The two faded bars above are the behaviour. Whatever is currently turning them into a colourless form is the fault.
2 · Today line. It must sit above the bars: its z-index is higher than any lane content, and the stacking context it lives in must not be created inside a lane. It is currently being painted behind cards.
3 · The header must not collide. The range is the left column of a auto 1fr auto grid; a Playfair 19px range grows with its content and squeezes the centre. Make the range a bounded width and give the centre column min-width:0 with the search at width:min(340px,100%), so the search shrinks rather than anything overlapping. Never let the three columns total more than the row.
4 · The caveat flag opens on click. It should reveal on hover and focus, like the board's other marks, with a short delay in and out so it doesn't flicker as the pointer crosses the lane.

My pick for the range: A, two leaves. The window's start and end as two 44px calendar leaves with an arrow between — the same leaf the cards and list already use, so the page gains no new object. It's a fixed 100px whatever the dates say, so the header can't be squeezed by a long month name, and it reads as "a window between two dates" faster than a sentence does. The mono caption under each leaf is unnecessary here; the month strip carries it.

B is a single pill — window then "30 Jul – 27 Oct" in Playfair 15px — which is compact and bounded but still varies by about 40px across the year. C sets the range in mono at 11px, which is the smallest and the most machine-like; it loses the warmth the header currently has. The current Playfair 19px is the thing to drop: it's the widest possible rendering of the most variable content in the row.
