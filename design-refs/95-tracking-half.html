Why this proportion is the right one
  • Tracking is the only card with a story. The other two are inventories — a manuscript with three materials, a note with a timestamp — and they were being given the same width as a narrative that has a beginning, a present and a future. Equal thirds flattered the short cards and starved the long one.
  • With half the pane, Tracking becomes a timeline. Sent → waiting → nudge, each with its date on the right rail, materials as chips under the event they belong to, and the progress bar reading against her stated window rather than floating free. That is the query's actual life, and it was previously three lines of text pretending to be a card.
  • Two stats lead it, in the comps page's grammar — icon plate, Playfair number, mono caption. 0 days waiting and reply expected ~8 Oct are the two facts a writer opens this page to check, so they sit above everything and read at a glance.
  • Stacking the right column suits what those cards are. Both are short and fixed-height by nature; stacked, they fill the same column height Tracking needs and neither trails off into white. What you sent gains a proper manuscript row and per-material sent marks; Notes gains its composer inline instead of below the fold.
  • Header bands carry a right-aligned meta — Queried · 3 items · 1 note — lifted from the comps page's 0 COMPS. Each card states its own count, so the body never has to.
  • Try Tracking slightly wider (1.15 / 0.85): the timeline breathes and the inventories stay legible. If chip rows wrap awkwardly at 1280 in the even split, this is the fallback.
