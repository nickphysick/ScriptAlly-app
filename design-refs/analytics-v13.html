Take the guesswork out of querying

Replies come back one at a time over months, and it is hard to see a pattern in a pile of letters. This page keeps all of your queries in one picture and updates it every time you record a reply, so you can decide what to send next on what has actually happened so far.

What the numbers can't tell you

Everything on this page rests on 27 queries and 10 replies

Small numbers move a lot

One more reply moves the request rate by about four percentage points. Treat the rates as a rough picture rather than a precise measure.

No reply is not counted as a no

Thirteen queries are still waiting. They are left out of the rates until they close, so the rates can go up or down from here.

Medians, not averages

A single very slow reply would pull an average a long way. The median is the middle value, so it is less affected by one outlier.

What this page cannot see

How full an agent's list is, what they have recently sold, whether they are on leave, and when your query was actually read. None of that is recorded here, and all of it affects the numbers.
