Questions

What happens when my six free months end?

You move onto the founding rate — half price, for as long as you keep querying with ScriptAlly. Nothing is charged automatically without telling you first.

What if I don't want to pay anything?

Stay on Free. One manuscript, unlimited agents and queries, the full timeline — it isn't a trial and it doesn't expire.

What happens when the hundred places are gone?

Founding access closes. Pro stays at £7 a month, and everyone who joined as a Founding Writer keeps their rate.
