Where it must not go — three ways the third colour breaks the system

✗ Not on the pillsThey'd read as a different kind of material
Hook-first One-page synopsis
The whole point of snapshot-not-reference is that these are ordinary materials — identical to hand-attached ones, individually editable. Colouring them says they are a special class that behaves differently. They are not.
✗ Not as a Pro badgeThe free plan shows no ceilings
◆ Pro feature
A locked law: no Pro badges, no meters, no upsell in the working surface. Blue can be the colour packages happen to wear; it must never be the colour that says you cannot have this.
✗ Not on the timelineDirection owns that axis

Burgundy is outgoing, sage is incoming — rings are a locked vocabulary. A blue ring would read as a third direction that does not exist.
Blue gets one meaning: this came from a template you built. That is a different axis from the two the app already uses, which is exactly why it can be added without collision — burgundy is outgoing, sage is incoming, and provenance is orthogonal to direction.
