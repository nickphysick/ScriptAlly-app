The band head carries the type, so the card body never repeats it — the name gets the Playfair line to itself. Package cards list their slots as rows rather than a comma string, which is what makes a package look like it contains things. Colour is doing real work here: at a glance the shelf tells you you're letter-heavy and short on samples.
