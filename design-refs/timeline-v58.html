Design of record — v58. One white card per relationship spanning its current wait on the cream field; chip states the status (imperative only when already overdue); the bar's end wears a terminal mark (diamond = your deadline, ring = estimate or schedule) and an action cap centred on the date. Overdue cards wobble occasionally instead of carrying a pattern. Journeys are the slim track and nodes; tasks are points with rolled ghosts; text lives in the card and glides on hover when clipped; the full record is the hover tip. Fixed three-month window, today centred, scrolled by whole weeks; ruler header; counts derived from the rows.

Agency estimates are stated, never judged: only writer-owed dates count as overdue. Dashed frames mean closed, and nothing else.
