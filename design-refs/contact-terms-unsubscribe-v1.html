Contact, Terms, Unsubscribe — redesign v1

What's wrong with these pages today

Read from the dev line. Privacy was redesigned on 2 November and is the best of the set; the others should come up to it.

Terms

  1. It's a public page with placeholders in it. The first clause reads "an agreement between you and [LEGAL ENTITY NAME] of [REGISTERED / TRADING ADDRESS]", and a ribbon says the terms are a working draft that hasn't been legally reviewed.
  2. It's still in the old document layout. Privacy has the navy header, "The short version" and an "On this page" list; Terms has none of them.
  3. Dated 31 October, so it predates the plan rulings: 14-day trial, plans rolling on to monthly, Smart Import once.

Contact

  1. The form doesn't send. "Send message" opens the visitor's own mail app with the message pasted in, then shows "sent". Anyone without a mail app set up sends nothing and is told they did.
  2. Six topics in a drop-down, and none changes anything. A bug report, a data request and a founding-access question all get the same empty box.
  3. "Billing or account" is offered while nothing is billed.
  4. Three blocks of advice sit beside the form repeating what the topic list already says.
  5. A small "Contact" label sits above "Get in touch.", the eyebrow style you've ruled out.

Unsubscribe

  1. It doesn't look like QueryHawk. System fonts, a burgundy link, no logo.
  2. There's no way back. One mistaken click gives up a founding place, and the page offers only an email address.
  3. It only knows the waiting list. App emails (nudge reminders, weekly summary) will need their own version with the three switches.

Footer and cookie bar

  1. The cookie bar is dead code. It asks about Google Analytics, which you dropped; it can no longer appear. It and the footer's "Cookie choices" link should be removed.
  2. The footer is sound. The row of status dots under the tagline means nothing to a visitor who hasn't used the app; I'd drop it.
