Build notes
Asset: design/refreshed-designs/icons/Goal_Reached.png, transparent PNG at 208×208 for retina, rendered at 104. If it ships with a white field it needs mix-blend-mode:multiply — and then no transform on any ancestor, including the entrance animation. In that case switch the keyframe to top.

Height: +101px on the rail. The activity card's min-height (150px) has to absorb it — it must be free to shrink, or this state pushes the dashboard past the fold. Worth a measured check at 1440×900 and 1440×720 as part of the build.

Entrance: fires once, on the transition into the state — not on every dashboard mount. A remount-triggered replay is what makes this kind of thing grating. Gate it on the reached-date being today and honour prefers-reduced-motion.

Subject for your illustrator: a dart landed in the target, or a full out-tray of sent letters. Desk-and-correspondence set, hard-edged palette, burgundy/blush for outgoing.
