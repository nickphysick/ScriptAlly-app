Primary verbs, per kind. Offer → Action opens the offer flow (accept, decline, notify the others). Agent waiting → Action opens the send flow. R&R → Action opens the resubmission flow. Deadline → Review opens the query. Sweeps → Start walks the cohort one at a time. Stale → Close (the one-minute act). Your tasks and notes → no primary; the tick is the act. Snoozed → Return. Done → Undo. Dismissed → Restore.
Who may be dismissed. Offers and deadlines never — the slot renders empty so the absence is visible, not mysterious. Sweeps dismiss as a cohort with a confirm. Stale and housekeeping dismiss with a light confirm. Your own tasks and notes get Delete in the ⋯ instead, with an eight-second undo, because they are the only things you own outright.
Snooze ceilings. Offers: tomorrow. Deadlines: never past the deadline — the dial physically stops there. Everything else: a year. The dial always names the resulting date in Playfair before you commit, and a snoozed item returns to Needs you now on its day carrying a BACK TODAY chip for that day only.

5 · Toasts and reversal
Four shapes, one position, one undo.
Done. Reply to Curtis Vane cleared.UndoSnoozed until Tuesday 11 August.UndoSending the full is what completes this.Open it7 closed. Six queries updated, one needed a note.Undo all
One toast, one undo, always in the same place — bottom-left, above the fold, 8 seconds, pausing on hover and never stacking: a second act replaces the first rather than queueing beneath it. Batch acts report their shape honestly (“six updated, one needed a note”) rather than claiming a clean sweep. The warning toast is the only one that ever appears in pink, and only when an act was refused for a reason worth learning.

Selection begins the moment you tick a checkbox or press X; checkboxes materialise across every row and the bar rises from the foot. It states what it holds (“all stale”) and offers only verbs valid for every member — a mixed selection loses Close them rather than half-applying it. Offers are excluded automatically and the bar says so. One toast, one undo, for the whole batch.

Below 900px the grid gives up its columns and each row becomes a small card. Title and subtitle lead; the status pill, the age and the two verbs that matter sit on a second line. The journey meter is dropped rather than shrunk — five pixels of progress in a 90px column tells nobody anything; the stage is already named in the pill row.

Swipe replaces the icon verbs: right to tick, left to reveal snooze and dismiss, with the same ceilings and the same single undo. The dial opens as a bottom sheet with the same snap stops, because a 17px knob is not a touch target but a full-width track is.

Controls stack. Search collapses into an icon; sort and Add join a single row beneath the stat chips; Work the list becomes a floating action at the foot, where the thumb is.
