Privacy

The short version

  • Your querying records and your writing belong to you.

  • We don’t sell it, and we don’t run advertising.
  • AI only does the admin. It never writes for you, and nothing you store trains it.

1Who we are

QueryHawk is run by Nick Physick, an independent developer based in the United Kingdom (“we”, “us”). We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy, and you can reach us at hello@queryhawk.net.

2What we collect

  • Account details — your email address, display name, and authentication credentials, handled by Firebase Authentication.
  • Your querying records — the agents, queries, manuscripts, submission packages, notes, and activity history you create in the app.
  • Documents you import — files you choose to upload through Smart Import (for example a querying spreadsheet), processed as described in section 4.
  • Waitlist emails — if you join the waitlist on our holding page, the email address you give us.
  • Support correspondence — anything you send us by email.

We do not collect payment details at present. If paid plans launch, payments will be handled by a payment processor and this policy will be updated first.

3How we use it, and on what basis

  • To run the service — storing and displaying your records is the entire product (performance of a contract).
  • To keep it working and secure — authentication, abuse prevention, and diagnosing faults (legitimate interests).
  • To reply to you — when you contact us or join the waitlist (legitimate interests / consent).

We do not sell personal data, share it with advertisers, or send marketing you haven’t asked for.

4How we use AI

AI does the admin, never the art. QueryHawk uses AI models only for menial, behind-the-scenes tasks: reading and sorting information you give it, so you don’t have to retype it. It never writes your query letters, synopses or pages, never edits your work, and never creates or designs anything for you.

AI is used only when you run one of these features, never in the background:

  • Smart Import — reads the spreadsheet or notes you upload and turns them into your agents and queries.
  • Smart Email Drop — reads an email you paste in and suggests the updates to make. Nothing changes until you accept them.
  • Comparable title suggestions — looks at the details you’ve entered about your manuscript and suggests published titles you might compare it to.
  • Finding agent details — looks up an agent’s public submission information and shows you where each detail came from, for you to check.

These tasks are processed on our behalf by Anthropic (the maker of the Claude AI models). Under the commercial terms we use, Anthropic does not use this data to train its models. The results are stored in your account; the processing itself is transient.

Your manuscripts, pitches and notes stored in QueryHawk are not sent to any AI service unless you run one of the features above.

5Where your data lives

Your data is stored on Google’s Firebase platform (Firestore, Authentication, and Hosting) in the London (europe-west2) region. Google acts as our data processor under its Cloud Data Processing Addendum. Where any processing involves a transfer outside the UK, it is protected by recognised safeguards such as the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

6Cookies and local storage

QueryHawk uses only what’s strictly necessary to work: authentication tokens that keep you signed in, and local storage that remembers interface preferences (like a collapsed sidebar) on your own device. There are no advertising cookies and no cross-site trackers, which is why you won’t find a cookie banner here. We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which doesn’t use cookies or track you across sites.

When you join the founding writers’ waiting list, we note which link brought you to the site (for example, our Instagram page), so we know where our writers come from. It’s stored with your sign-up and nowhere else.

7Retention and deletion

Your records are kept for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, your data is deleted from our systems within 30 days, allowing for backup cycles.

If you join the founding writers’ list we keep your email address, the page you signed up from, and a one-way hash of your IP address — we never store the address itself. We use it only to send you founding-access email: a confirmation, and your invite. It is never sold, never shared, and never used for anything else.

You can leave at any time using the unsubscribe link in any of those emails. It takes effect immediately and frees your founding place. We also delete a waitlist entry automatically 24 months after we last heard from you, and within 30 days of you unsubscribing. If you would rather we removed you sooner, email hello@queryhawk.net and we will.

8Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent where consent is the basis. Email hello@queryhawk.net with “Privacy” in the subject line and we’ll respond within one month.

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk — though we’d appreciate the chance to put it right first.

9Children

QueryHawk is not directed at children and is intended for users aged 16 and over.

10Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in any way that matters, we’ll update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app or by email before they take effect.
