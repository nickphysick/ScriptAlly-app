The gap is real: a reply that decides nothing currently has nowhere to go. An acknowledgement, "still reading", "sorry, two more weeks", "can you resend as a PDF" — all of these are contact from the agent, none of them move the query. Today you would have to either record nothing, and watch the tracker keep counting a silence that has ended, or record a false stage change.

⚠️ The counting distinction is the one to get right. A holding reply is contact, not an answer — the query is still open and undecided, so it must stay on the unanswered side of the count and stay in its list group. If it silently moved to "answered", the number that tells you how your querying is going would start including agents who have decided nothing.

The new timeframe is theirs, not yours. It arrives from their message, so it renders with their attribution — Aisha advises 2 weeks — under the same rule that separates the agency's claim from your own estimate. ⚠️ It should be stored on the reply event, not written back to the agent record: two weeks was what she said about this manuscript in August, not the agency's standing policy.
