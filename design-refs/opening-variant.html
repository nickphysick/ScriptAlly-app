The amendment

One optional label
Sample materials gain an optional opening variant label — free text, suggested from what you've used before. It rides through packages into tracking, and the same label goes on a full request. Nothing else changes: no version records, no second manuscript library, no new collection.

4 · When a full is requested

The consistency check
18 AUG ← Full manuscript requested by R. Osei §Read: Prologue-first
19 AUG → Full manuscript sent to R. Osei §Sent: Prologue-first ✓ matches what they read
The Read chip is derived — it's the variant on the sample in the package that went out with that query, so the app already knows it. The Sent chip is the one thing the writer records, and it defaults to the read variant.

If they differ, the row says so plainly — △ differs from what they read — and stops there. It's a fact worth seeing, not a mistake worth scolding: sending a revised opening can be deliberate.
