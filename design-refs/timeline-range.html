Enhanced — spine, ground, boundaries

Bars are 44px now. Drag the range from one week to six months: the labels leave before the bars do, the column grain coarsens from days to weeks to months, and at the long end the row head takes over the sentence the bar can no longer hold.
Range 1 week
Four density tiers, not a smooth shrink. 1–2 weeks: day columns, full labels in the bars, marker captions shown. 1 month: day columns, labels still fit, captions smaller. 3 months: week columns, bars lose their text entirely and become pure shape — the row head carries the sentence instead. 6 months: month columns, bars slimmer again, markers reduced to their smallest legible size, task chips become squares.

Why the row head takes over. A bar's label is only readable while the bar is wide enough to hold it. Rather than truncating to "Your mo…", the sentence moves to the one place whose width never changes. Nothing is lost; it relocates.

What the long view is actually for. Not detail — shape. Six months shows a whole submission round in one screen: where the sends clustered, how long the silences ran, which relationships are still open. That is a question the week view cannot answer at all.
