Legal · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 Aug 2026

The short version: your querying records and your writing belong to you. We collect only what the app needs to work, we don't sell it, we don't run advertising, and nothing you store is used to train AI models.

01

Who we are

ScriptAlly is operated by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME] of [REGISTERED / TRADING ADDRESS], United Kingdom ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. You can reach us at hello@scriptally.ink. [ICO registration number: pending.]

02

What we collect

  • Account details — your email address, display name, and authentication credentials, handled by Firebase Authentication.
  • Your querying records — the agents, queries, manuscripts, submission packages, notes, and activity history you create in the app.
  • Documents you import — files you choose to upload through Smart Import (for example a querying spreadsheet), processed as described in section 4.
  • Waitlist emails — if you join the waitlist on our holding page, the email address you give us.
  • Support correspondence — anything you send us by email.

We do not collect payment details at present. If paid plans launch, payments will be handled by a payment processor and this policy will be updated first.

03

How we use it, and on what basis

  • To run the service — storing and displaying your records is the entire product (performance of a contract).
  • To keep it working and secure — authentication, abuse prevention, and diagnosing faults (legitimate interests).
  • To reply to you — when you contact us or join the waitlist (legitimate interests / consent).

We do not sell personal data, share it with advertisers, or send marketing you haven't asked for.

04

Smart Import and AI processing

When you use Smart Import, the contents of the file you upload are transmitted to Anthropic (the maker of the Claude AI models), which processes them on our behalf to extract your agents and queries into structured records. This happens only when you actively run an import — never in the background.

Under the commercial API terms we use, Anthropic does not use this data to train its models. The extracted records are stored in your account; the processing itself is transient.

Your manuscripts, pitches, and notes stored in ScriptAlly are not sent to any AI service unless you explicitly run a feature that says so on its face.

05

Where your data lives

Your data is stored on Google's Firebase platform (Firestore, Authentication, and Hosting) in the [europe-west2 — London] region. Google acts as our data processor under its Cloud Data Processing Addendum. Where any processing involves a transfer outside the UK, it is protected by recognised safeguards such as the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

06

Cookies and local storage

ScriptAlly uses only what's strictly necessary to work: authentication tokens that keep you signed in, and local storage that remembers interface preferences (like a collapsed sidebar) on your own device. There are no advertising cookies, no cross-site trackers, and no third-party analytics — which is why you won't find a cookie banner here.

07

Retention and deletion

Your records are kept for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, your data is deleted from our systems within [30] days, allowing for backup cycles. Waitlist emails are deleted once launch invitations have gone out or on request, whichever is sooner.

08

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent where consent is the basis. Email hello@scriptally.ink with "Privacy" in the subject line and we'll respond within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk — though we'd appreciate the chance to put it right first.

09

Children

ScriptAlly is not directed at children and is intended for users aged 16 and over.

10

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in any way that matters, we'll update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app or by email before they take effect.
