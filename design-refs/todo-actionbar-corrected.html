What changed, and why the old one failed. Sticky positioning can only hold a bar over the scrolling page — the content still exists behind and below it, and it reappears in whatever strip sits beneath. The corrected pane doesn't scroll at all. The worksheet card owns a scroller inside its rim; the rim has overflow: hidden, so work vanishes at the card's edge and there is nothing underneath to reappear. The bar is the card's sibling, not a lid over it.

Alignment is now structural rather than coincidental. The grid row is align-items: stretch; the pane column is a flex column of three parts — header (fixed), worksheet (flex: 1, shrinks and scrolls), bar (fixed, last). The column therefore always fills the row's height exactly, so the bar's bottom edge and the record card's bottom edge are the same line by construction. Try toggle 1: they end together, with no shadow tricks holding them there.

The record scrolls its own story the same way if a long history would outgrow the pane — header and the query link stay put, the middle scrolls, and the card still ends where the bar ends.

The result: one screen, no page scroll, two columns that always finish on the same line, and a bar that is never a lid.
