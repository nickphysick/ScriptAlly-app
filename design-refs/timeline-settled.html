The two new glyphs. ! marks a nudge falling due — it points at something you can do, so it reads as a prompt rather than a scolding. The clock marks a query gone quiet: it reports elapsed time and nothing else, which is why it can carry weight without becoming a verdict. Both are circles, so both are clickable; the notch stays straight-sided, because nothing is behind it.

Every bar ends at something nameable. An expected reply, a reminder, a deadline, a disc — or today, where nothing is known. Nothing is drawn past a decision the writer has not made.
