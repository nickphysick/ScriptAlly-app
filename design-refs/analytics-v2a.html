What these numbers can't tell you

Every figure on this page rests on 27 queries and 12 replies.

Rates move on one reply

The request rate shifts by roughly four percentage points each time one more agent answers. Read it as a shape, not a score.

Silence isn't a no

13 queries are still out. They sit outside the request rate until they close, so it can move in either direction.

Median, not average

One reply after two years would drag an average out of shape. The median shows the middle of the pack.

None of this is about the book alone

How full an agent's list is, what sold last season, who's on leave, whether it landed on the right desk in the right week — none of it is visible from your side.
