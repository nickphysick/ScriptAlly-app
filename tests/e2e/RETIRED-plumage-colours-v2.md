# Retired by Plumage colours v3

`pc2.measure.ts` (PC2-1 … PC2-9, PC2-F) is deleted. Plumage colours v3 (`design-refs/plumage-colours-v3/`) took the
stripes off the offer, put them on Revise & Resubmit, moved the grid card's label to its top edge at the right,
restored the frame's clipping and replaced the paperclip with a materials tile. Its locks are `pc3.measure.ts`.

| v2 lock | what it measured | now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PC2-1 | no with-you outline on a band, strip, bubble or Birds-eye bar | carried: PC3-3 (grid, slide-in, Birds-eye) |
| PC2-2 | no v1 Full or navy-offer hex on the page | carried and widened: PC3-3 sweep (adds v2's #dbe4ee and #f6f0e5) |
| PC2-3 | the band per status (R&R and Resubmitted on #b9c9da) | replaced: PC3-3 (R&R flat #d3deea off the card bands) |
| PC2-4 | the offer's stripes on two card bands, flat elsewhere | replaced: PC3-1 (no gradient on any offer surface) and PC3-2 (the R&R stripes) |
| PC2-5 | ink on every offer surface; contrast on duck egg | carried: PC3-1 (ink), PC3-9 (the agency line ≥ 4.5:1 on every band) |
| PC2-6 / 6W | the label on the band's bottom left, unclipped | replaced: PC3-4 / PC3-4W (top right, outside the frame, frame clipping on) |
| PC2-7 | the label clear of seal, name and facts; one card height | replaced: PC3-5 (adds the track and the group heading above) |
| PC2-8 | no "With you", chevron or new arrow | carried: PC3-6 |
| PC2-9 | the slide-in rim for Full and Offer; (b) the four search tiles | replaced: PC3-10 (the Full rim; the agent tile is v3's) |
| PC2-F | the frames | replaced: PC3-F and PC3-W |

Also re-pointed by v3: `qc157.measure.ts` QC157-1 (an R&R band is checked striped, the offer flat through
statusBand), `rs15.measure.ts` D1 (the label, not the paperclip, is what rises above a Responses card), and
`qc154.measure.ts` M4 (the paperclip's pop-up) is retired in place for PC3-8 and PC3-11.
