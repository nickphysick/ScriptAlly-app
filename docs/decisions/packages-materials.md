- **⚠️ SIDE BY SIDE IS FACTS ONLY (D6)** — shown with ≥2 sent, rows in the list's own order, no row styled differently, no rank or verdict words anywhere on the page. `strongestPackage`/`rank*` are not imported.

A well accepts only its own kind by calling `preventDefault` in `dragover` only for that kind.
