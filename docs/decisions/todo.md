### From “Conventions & invariants” (CLAUDE.md lines 749–750)

- **⚠️ A HEADING MAY NOT CARRY COLOUR-SHIFTED EMPHASIS — emphasis is ITALIC, in the heading's own ink.** *(To-do task pane, 21 Aug; asked for repeatedly before it was written down, which is why it is a law and not a preference.)* `.deed em` was burgundy for three rounds. **A heading that changes colour mid-sentence reads as two things**, and the reader has to work out which half is the point — which is the opposite of what a heading is for. The rule is structural, not a value to remember: the emphasis element declares **`color: inherit`**, so it cannot drift even if someone retones the theme. **The check is a COVERAGE assertion** (`deedRound.measure.ts` P1.2): it walks every heading-role element in the pane and **every descendant of each**, comparing computed colour against the heading's own — because nobody puts the offending span at the top level, and a first-level check is how a rule like this survives for years. ⚠️ **The same edit had to fix `line-height: 1.12`**, which the standing Playfair-descender rule already forbade — mixed-case Playfair needs 1.3, and a deed that is now a whole sentence is full of descenders.
- **⚠️ A BACKGROUND TINT INSIDE THE FORM CARD MEANS THE THING IS TOUCHABLE.** *(To-do task pane, 21 Aug; `design-refs/todo-pane-contract.html` carries the same sentence.)* The card is **white throughout**. Tint survives on exactly five things and every one is something the writer can act on: **inputs, textareas, unchosen segment buttons (`--field`), chosen buttons and pills (`--pink`), and the expanded quantity**. Nothing else — no wrapper box, no wash, no band, no card-within-card. **Sections are separated by a 1px `--hair` rule, last one none**, because a rule between two sections says "these are different questions" and says nothing else, while a bordered fill around two of them says "region" and invites a third meaning. **The fault this retires:** the expectations block was a field-tinted bordered box around two questions, so the page taught that tint means *grouped* as well as *touchable* — and once it means two things it means neither. **The check is a computed-background enumeration over EVERY descendant of the form card, not the first level** (`steerRound.measure.ts` P4.2), with the population asserted first so an empty scan cannot pass.
